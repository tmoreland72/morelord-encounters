# Morelord Encounters: Player Guide

Morelord Encounters is a GM encounter builder. Players do not configure monster sources, encounter difficulty, encounter stories, or module settings. The GM uses these tools to prepare what happens at the table.

## Player participation

Keep your character's level and statistics current. The GM can include your character in the encounter party; that selection contributes to the difficulty estimate. It does not move your token, roll initiative, or change your character.

The GM presents scenes, places creatures, and shares appropriate handouts. Encounters does not automatically request player rolls for guided scenes. Follow the GM's instructions and use your normal Foundry character controls when asked to roll.

## Pages and controls

There are currently no player-editable Encounters forms or settings. The builder, configuration window, saved encounter library, and story authoring controls are GM tools. Player-facing campaign documents are handouts the GM explicitly shares.

| Visible control or content | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **Documentation** | Opens the module's documentation on the Morelord Gaming website in a new browser tab. Use the website's nested navigation to select sections and guides. |
| **Shared journal page / handout** | Campaign content shared by the GM. Access follows Foundry document permissions; private GM notes are not revealed automatically. |

## Troubleshooting

If a shared handout cannot be opened, ask the GM to check its player permissions. Private encounter rosters and story notes remain GM-only; reading a product manual does not expose them.

The [GM Manual](gm-manual.md) explains installation, configuration, encounter-builder controls, and story workflows.
