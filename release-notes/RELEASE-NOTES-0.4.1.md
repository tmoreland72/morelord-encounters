# Morelord Encounters 0.4.1

## What Changed

### Changed

- **The Horde** now picks the strongest creature that fits ten times within the XP budget. It is still ten of the same creature from your enabled sources, and you get a warning only when no creature fits ten times.
- **Elite Team** keeps one rival per selected character, not counting companions, but lowers the Challenge Ratings until the team fits the XP budget. Each roll can change the creatures. You get a warning only when even the lowest Challenge Ratings cannot fit, and no rival is dropped.

### Fixed

- Rerolling a Horde creature no longer flags the ten-creature roster as larger than the party.
- Rerolling an Elite Team creature keeps the budget warning when the lowest ratings still do not fit.

### Requires

- Morelord Core 0.7.0 or later.
