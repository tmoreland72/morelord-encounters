# Unreleased

## What Changed

### Changed

- **The Horde** is still ten of the same creature from your enabled sources. Each roll now chooses among the creatures at the highest Challenge Rating whose ten-fold XP fits the budget, so the horde lands near that budget. You get a warning only when no creature fits ten times.
- **Elite Team** (Mirror Team) is still one rival per selected character, not counting companions. Rivals start near each character's level, then their Challenge Ratings step down evenly until the total fits the XP budget. Each roll can change the creatures. You get a warning only when even the lowest Challenge Ratings cannot fit, and no rival is dropped.

### Fixed

- Rerolling a Horde creature keeps the ten-creature roster from being flagged as larger than the party. Rerolling a mirror-team creature keeps the budget warning when the lowest ratings still do not fit.
